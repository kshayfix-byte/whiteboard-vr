Board size

Pick a size for this whiteboard. It changes for everyone in the room, in 2D and in VR.

Width 0.5–4 m, height 0.5–2.5 m. Drawings keep their size and stay in the middle: the board grows or shrinks evenly on every side. Shrinking hides drawings past the new edge, and they come back if you make the board bigger again.
